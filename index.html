HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS
MouseSteer your snake
SpaceBoost (drains length)
EscapePause the game
JoystickMove (mobile)
Boost buttonBoost (mobile)
OBJECTIVE

Collect food to grow longer. If your head hits another snake's body, you are eliminated. Get in front of enemies and let them run into you.

BOOST

Hold Boost to move faster. It drains your length while active. Use it to escape or cut off an enemy. You cannot boost below length 10.

COINS
Every 10 orbs+1 Coin
Defeat a snake+25 Coins
Survive 1 minute+20 Coins
Length bonus+length / 10 Coins

Use coins in the Shop to unlock new skins.

MAPS
Void NexusSpace arena, default map
Crimson DepthsVolcanic arena with ember effects
Abyssal SeaOcean floor with soft glow effects
TIPS
  • Bigger snakes move slower. Stay small if you want to dodge more easily.
  • Circle around smaller snakes to trap them.
  • Check the mini-map to spot nearby snakes.
  • Use a short boost to grab an orb before an enemy gets there.

Privacy Policy
Last Updated: July 11, 2026
1. Introduction

Snake Dominion takes your privacy seriously. This page explains what data the game stores, how it is used, and what control you have over it.

2. Information We Collect

Snake Dominion runs entirely in your browser. All data is stored locally on your device using Local Storage. Nothing is sent to a server. The following data is saved:

  • Game Scores: your best score and session statistics.
  • Player Settings: your name, selected skin, chosen map, and audio or graphics preferences.
  • In-Game Currency: your coin balance and unlocked skins.
  • Achievements: the achievements you have unlocked.

The game does not require your real name, email address, or location.

3. Use of Local Storage

Snake Dominion uses Local Storage to save your progress between sessions. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. The keys used are:

  • sd_best: your best score
  • sd_skin: your selected skin
  • sd_coins: your coin balance
  • sd_name: your player name
  • sd_map: your selected map
  • sd_ach: your achievement progress
  • sd_owned_skins: skins you have unlocked

You can delete all saved data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Cookies

Snake Dominion does not use cookies. All data persistence relies on Local Storage as described above.

5. Third-Party Services

Snake Dominion may load the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: Used to load the Fraunces and Work Sans typefaces. Google may log your IP address when serving these fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Google AdSense: If ads are shown, Google AdSense may collect anonymized data and use cookies for ad personalization. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings.
  • Google Analytics: We may use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used. No personally identifiable data is collected. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.
6. Data Security

Snake Dominion does not transmit any data over the internet. All game data lives on your device. We cannot be responsible for unauthorized access caused by a compromised device or browser.

7. Your Rights

All data is stored locally, so you have full control over it. You can:

  • View saved data in your browser's developer tools under Application > Local Storage.
  • Delete all game data by clearing Local Storage or site data for this page.
  • Reset preferences from the in-game Settings screen.
8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@snakedominion.xyz or through the Contact page in the game.

About the Game

Snake Dominion is a browser-based arena game built on the classic snake formula. You fight for position against 100 AI-controlled snakes, collecting food to grow longer and taking out smaller snakes to earn coins and climb the scoreboard.

Built entirely with vanilla JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. No game engine, no external dependencies. All visuals and audio are generated at runtime.

Key Features
🐍
Arena Combat
Compete against 100 AI snakes. The controls are smooth and the AI improves on harder difficulty settings.
🗺
3 Maps
Void Nexus, Crimson Depths, and Abyssal Sea. Each map has its own visual style and particle effects.
🎨
16 Skins
Earn coins and unlock different snake skins from the shop. Skins are purely cosmetic.
🪙
Coins and Shop
Earn coins during each match by collecting food, defeating snakes, and surviving. Spend them in the shop.
📱
Mobile Support
Works on mobile browsers with a virtual joystick and boost button. No app install needed.
⚡
Boost
Hold boost to move faster. It drains your length while active, so use it at the right moment.
